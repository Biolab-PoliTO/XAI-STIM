Introduction ▼

What is this tool?
This is a validation tool for reviewing automatic STN (SubThalamic Nucleus) detection from microelectrode recordings (MERs). It uses AI to identify STN regions and shows you explanations of how the AI made its decisions.

What You'll Do:

  1. Mark regions on MER signals where you see STN activity
  2. Review the AI's automatic detection and explanations
  3. Provide feedback on the AI's performance
  4. Export your annotations and responses
Screenshot: Main Interface Overview

Getting Started ▼

Requirements:

  • Web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended)
  • MER trajectory files in JSON format
  • Basic familiarity with MER signal interpretation

Opening the Tool:

  1. Locate the XAI_STIM_validation_tool.html file
  2. Double-click to open it in your browser
  3. The tool runs entirely in your browser - no installation needed
Screenshot: Opening the Tool

Step-by-Step Workflow ▼

Load Data → Profile → Select Trajectory → Annotate → Review AI → Survey → Repeat → Export

Step 1: Load Your Data

Click "Select Trajectory Files" and choose your JSON files. You can select multiple files at once.
Screenshot: Loading Data Files

Once loaded, you'll see statistics showing the number of trajectories and questions. Click "Start Study" to begin.

Screenshot: Data Successfully Loaded

Step 2: Enter Your Profile

Provide basic information about yourself:

  • Participant ID: Auto-filled with current date/time (you can modify it)
  • Years of Experience: Your experience with MER interpretation
  • Role: Select your professional role
Screenshot: Profile Information Form

Answer 6 Yes/No questions about your experience with AI systems.

Screenshot: AI Attitude Questions

Step 3: Select a Trajectory

Choose which trajectory you want to annotate. Click on any trajectory to select it (it will turn green), then click "Annotate Trajectory".

Screenshot: Trajectory Selection
Tip: Completed trajectories are marked with ✓ and grayed out. The interface shows your progress (e.g., "2 of 5 completed").
GIF: Selecting a Trajectory

Step 4: Annotate the Signal (Blind Review)

Your Task: Mark regions where you identify STN-typical activity by clicking and dragging on the signal plot.
Screenshot: Annotation Interface

Understanding the Plot:

  • X-axis: Time in seconds
  • Y-axis: Depth in millimeters
  • Each line: One recording channel at a specific depth

Creating Annotations:

  1. Make sure ✏️ Annotation Mode is checked
  2. Click and drag across the time period showing STN activity
  3. A green rectangle appears marking your annotation
  4. Repeat for all STN regions you identify
GIF: Creating an Annotation
STN Features to Look For:
  • High-frequency oscillations (300-3000 Hz)
  • Irregular, dense spike activity
  • Increased background noise

Useful Controls:

  • Amplitude Scale slider: Adjust vertical scaling to see signals better
  • 🧽 Erase Mode: Click on annotations to delete them
  • Clear All button: Remove all annotations (with confirmation)
Screenshot: Annotation Controls
GIF: Adjusting Amplitude Scale

Your annotations appear in a list below the plot. When finished, click "Save & Continue".

Step 5: Review AI Classification (Informed Review)

Now you'll see: The AI's automatic classification with visual explanations showing where the AI focused to make its decision.
Screenshot: XAI Review Interface

Understanding the Visualization:

1. Heatmap (Score-CAM):

  • Red/Yellow (warm) colors: Regions that strongly influenced the AI's decision
  • Blue/Purple (cool) colors: Regions with less influence
  • This shows where the AI "looked" to classify the trajectory
Screenshot: Heatmap with Annotations

2. Probability Bars (right side):

  • Green bars: High STN probability (>50%)
  • Gold bars: Medium probability (45-50%)
  • Red bars: Low probability (<45%)
  • Numbers show exact probability for each depth
Screenshot: Probability Bars Close-up

3. Your Original Annotations:

Green rectangles show your annotations from the previous step. You can toggle them on/off to compare with the AI.

Interactive Controls:

  • 🔥 Show XAI Heatmap: Toggle the colored overlay on/off
  • Heatmap Opacity: Adjust transparency to see signals underneath
  • 🟩 Show My Annotations: Show/hide your annotations
  • ✏️ Annotation Mode: You can add or remove annotations after seeing the AI
Screenshot: XAI Controls Panel
GIF: Toggling Heatmap and Annotations
What to Look For:
  • Do the AI's highlighted regions match your annotations?
  • Is the AI focusing on relevant STN features?
  • Are there discrepancies between AI and your expert judgment?

After reviewing, you can refine your annotations. Any changes are saved separately as "informed annotations."

Step 6: Complete the Survey

Rate your agreement with 4 statements about the AI's performance for this trajectory.

After completing the survey, you'll return to the trajectory selection screen. Select the next trajectory and repeat steps 3-6 until all trajectories are completed.

Screenshot: Progress Tracking

After completing all trajectories, you'll answer 6 overall questions about your experience with the AI system across all cases.

Review your statistics and download your results as a JSON file.

Tips & Troubleshooting ▼

Best Practices:

  • Work in a quiet environment with a good quality monitor
  • Take breaks between trajectories to maintain focus
  • Use the amplitude scale to better visualize low-amplitude signals
  • Mark only high-confidence STN regions initially
  • Compare the AI's focus with your clinical judgment

Common Issues:

Problem: Can't create annotations
Solution: Make sure ✏️ Annotation Mode is checked and 🧽 Erase Mode is unchecked. Try clicking and dragging more deliberately on the signal lines.
Problem: Heatmap not visible
Solution: Check that "🔥 Show XAI Heatmap" is enabled and increase the Heatmap Opacity slider.
Problem: Survey won't submit
Solution: Scroll through all questions to ensure every one is answered. Each question requires a selection.
Problem: Files won't load
Solution: Ensure your JSON files are properly formatted. Try loading one file at a time to identify which file might have an issue.

Quick Reference Checklist:

  • Load trajectory files (JSON format)
  • Complete participant profile
  • Select first trajectory
  • Create blind annotations
  • Review AI explanation
  • Refine annotations if needed
  • Complete trajectory survey
  • Repeat for all trajectories
  • Complete final survey
  • Download results

Important Notes:

  • All processing happens in your browser - no data is sent to external servers
  • Use the navigation buttons in the interface (don't use browser back button)
  • Download results before closing the browser tab
  • Data is not saved between sessions - complete your study in one sitting or download partial results
